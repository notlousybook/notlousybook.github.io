# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fellow developers and open source collaborators (primary), Discord and YouTube community (secondary). They browse for cool projects, gauge personality, and decide whether to connect or collab. Casual browsing on desktop and mobile, often late-night, looking for authentic energy not corporate polish.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for notlousybook, a 15-year-old self-taught developer. Exists to showcase projects, express personality, and invite collaboration. Success looks like a visitor leaving with a clear sense of who this person is and wanting to reach out on Discord.

## Brand Personality

Chaotic, honest, cozy. Self-deprecating without being insecure, playful without being try-hard. The voice of someone showing you their fun side projects at 2am. Three words: casual, self-deprecating, playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS/startup landing pages
- AI-generated-looking templates or cookie-cutter designs
- Overdesigned, bloated sites with performance-heavy cruft
- Corporate minimalism that strips all personality

## Design Principles

1. **Authenticity over polish.** Handmade feel beats corporate sheen. Imperfect is honest.
2. **Playful darkness.** Dark theme that runs warm (teal-tinted neutrals), not cold or harsh.
3. **Every pixel earns its place.** No decoration for decoration's sake. If it doesn't communicate or delight, cut it.
4. **Show, don't tell.** Projects, code, and real demos speak louder than descriptors.
5. **Community-first.** Discord, collab, open source are front and center — not buried in a footer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Reduced motion query respected with animation disabling. Skip-to-content link present. Semantic HTML structure. Color contrast ratios maintained with OKLCH palette. Touch targets sized for mobile interaction.
